Case 01 · Rebranding · 2024

My Mithila, reimagined

greener strides
Client
My Mithila
Year
©2024
Scope
Rebranding · Identity · Logo · Packaging · E-commerce · Print

A purpose-led identity for My Mithila — a sustainable living brand turning everyday switches into a quiet movement.

Tifferend built the verbal voice, visual system, packaging and storefront that frame the founder's invitation: baby steps, greener strides.

Background

The mission needed a face.

My Mithila set out to make sustainable living approachable for everyday Indian households — but the category felt either preachy or premium-coded, with no warmth and no cultural anchor.

My Mithila wanted to feel like a friend who happened to care — credible, warm, and at home in an Indian kitchen. We started by clarifying what the brand really stood for, and steering hard away from the visual shorthand of greenwashing.

Strategy

Baby steps, greener strides.

We positioned My Mithila around a single, human idea: 'Baby Steps, Greener Strides.' A gentle, founder-led brand that celebrates small daily switches — sippers, bottles, kitchenware — rooted in the geometry and warmth of Mithila art.

The platform invites instead of lectures. Every product becomes a small switch; every switch a stride. We anchored the work around one line that became a north star for tone, packaging, and on-pack copy.

Verbal identity

"A simple man trying to lead a sustainable life. Found it extremely cool. Join me!"

Founder voice, printed on every business card

Visual identity

Rooted, but not earthy.

We stepped away from the kraft-and-sage cliché. The palette pairs a deep forest green with a sunlit mustard yellow — values that feel grown-up and joyful at once. Geometric Mithila motifs (triangle, circle, leaf, water) became a kit-of-parts that flexes from product tags to web hero, signed off by a confident lowercase wordmark.

  • MountainForest#0E5A2EPrimary — grounding green of the canopy
  • SunMustard#F5C518Accent — the sunlit warmth of intent
  • WaterBone#F4EFE6Canvas — the breath between marks
  • LeafInk#0A1F12Voice — the considered line of every mark

Brand toolkit

A kit, not a logo.

The wordmark is engineered on a strict grid — the lowercase 'm' carries the weight, the 'y' drops a counterform that echoes the leaf in the seal. Around it sits a flexible kit: sticker sheets for unboxings, a repeat pattern for inner liners and tape, and a hand-drawn seal that earns its place on every touchpoint.

Product experience

From the cart up.

Built a full identity system from logomark to packaging, business collateral, and an e-commerce storefront. The Mithila-inspired motifs became a kit-of-parts that flexes across product tags, social, and on-pack storytelling, anchored by a deep forest green and a sunlit mustard yellow.

The storefront leads with one new product family at a time — sippers, bottles, bamboo kitchenware — so the brand can scale category by category without ever losing the founder's voice. Every touchpoint was built to feel transparent, considered, and human.

Rebrand impact

Designing for purpose.

My Mithila launched with a cohesive D2C identity across web, print and social — instantly recognisable, founder-credible, and built to scale category-by-category as new products roll out.

This wasn't just better branding. It was alignment — of values, of vision, of voice — and a system the founder can carry into every new product, post and conversation.
