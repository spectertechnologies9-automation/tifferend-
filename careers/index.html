Our story — Know us better

Let's do thingstifferendly

Tifferend began with a simple idea: create a difference, with a difference. Our founder Kunj honed his skills in commercial photography, brand design, and media production while freelancing. Recognizing the power of combining these disciplines, he expanded to include social media marketing — always focused on solving real business problems, not just creating pretty content.

From those early days working with small budgets, Tifferend has grown to partner with a diverse range of brands — from local startups to established names. We've earned our clients' trust by consistently delivering results and going the extra mile to find innovative solutions.
